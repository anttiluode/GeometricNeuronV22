The geometric neuron, evolved

A useful working model treats a neuron as a spatially addressed, stateful dynamical medium with a severe readout bottleneck.

Inputs write locally. Morphology sets transfer and which regions can influence one another. Local nonlinear state changes what a write means. Downstream cells do not receive the full dendritic map; soma/axon dynamics project it into a much smaller output. So the computational degrees of freedom are not branches, modes or synapses — they are the internal differences that survive as materially distinct downstream futures.

run the accident and the evolved toy side by side →

The measurement Inject the same small input at address i, record the receiver trajectory h_i(t), and assemble H = [h_1 … h_K]. Normalise, then ask how many columns are actually distinguishable.
Why it matters A hundred anatomical locations are not a hundred channels. If they collapse to one normalised somatic response, the geometry bought nothing. Earned structure is when address and state produce genuinely different downstream futures.
Origin

What the accident actually was

Stated plainly, because the mythology around it has been expensive.

A PerceptionLab patch: a homeostatic coupler drove the square size of a checkerboard generator; the resulting image went into a 256-dimensional Image→Vector node, which spatially downsampled and flattened it; a vector splitter returned only outputs 0–3 to the coupler. The loop produced an ECG-like signal at some output dimensions and not others.

The V21 autopsy established the mechanism, and it is fully mechanical. int() on the square size is a quantizer, so the loop is a deterministic map on about fifty discrete cells driven by a variance thermostat. The plateau heights are the aliasing beat between board frequency and sampling grid. Spikes are the amplify branch's duty cycle. The tap test — which pixels the readout touches decides whether the loop lives or dies — reproduced on the real instrument.

None of that is a hidden image inside a neuron. What it did force into view was the combination of space, local write, limited readout, state and recurrence. That combination is the inheritance. The checkerboard was noise. The loop was the clue.

The stack

One pass through the medium

Read downward. Every stage can fail independently, which is the point of separating them.

  • ◦Slow context — gains, thresholds, what is possible at all
  • │Local write — synaptic input at an address
  • │Dendritic geometry — transfer, attenuation, compartmentalisation
  • │Propagation and mixing — what meets what, and when
  • │Local nonlinearity — the existing state decides what the write means

Ledger

Standing of every claim above

Scan the left column. Orange is what this repo killed, including things it wanted to be true.

  • Killed · V22

    Whole-tree spectral summaries are not extra computation

    Three frozen operator features on the structural graph — low-spectrum entropy, soma/root modal participation, low-mode spacing irregularity — looked spectacular against a size baseline of log area + log longest path. Then a one-bit species variable beat them, and under the paper's own matched-synapse condition they made prediction worse.

    original FCI target B2 .112 B2+G .755 species alone .796 matched-synapse target B2 .628 B2+G .608 species alone .006 MAE change -3.6% sign-flip p = .794

    The features were a morphological species fingerprint taking a shortcut through synaptic physics. This equation does not get resurrected by adding prettier graph features.

  • Killed · V13

    Passive geometry alone cannot prefer a direction

    Four separate attempts to get directed flow out of angled passive geometry returned a reciprocity ratio of 1.0000 to five decimals at every skew angle. The relevant constraint is reciprocity: a linear passive cable with symmetric couplings has symmetric transfer impedance even though it is dissipative. Shape alone does not create a preferred transfer direction.

    Consequence for this document: passive morphology can shape transfer, attenuation and delay, but a directional or non-additive effect needs something beyond symmetric linear coupling — for example state-dependent conductance, active dynamics, or an explicit coupling bias.

  • Measured · external

    Cable size and extent carry most of it, once physics is matched

    Aizenbud et al. (PNAS 2026) find total dendritic area is the single best morphological predictor of functional complexity, that bifurcation count is weak, and that the species gap survives equalising synaptic parameters. Their allocation result is the interesting part: length invested in bifurcation branches rather than trunk or terminal branches predicts higher complexity.

    bifurcation count R² .29 total dendritic area R² .74 + longest bifurcation branch R² .81 best triplet / quadruple R² .85 / .88 matched rat synapses, human still higher p = .022

    This is their result, not ours. It is the baseline any successor has to beat.

  • Open

    Does address plus state produce distinguishable receiver futures?

    The figure at the top is the measurement prescription; the companion simulator implements the toy version. Hold the input budget and the number of intervention sites fixed, vary the address, collect H, normalise per feature, and count what remains distinguishable. Then switch local nonlinearity on and ask how much the distinguishable set changes.

    Cable theory already describes location-to-location transfer through Green's functions; that part is old. What is not settled is whether the effective dimensionality of that transfer dictionary differs across real reconstructed trees after total area, total length and path statistics are matched.

  • Bet

    Slow structure edits the fast control surface

    The proposal is that slow variables do not learn a replacement computation. They move where the gate sits, how long it is, which routes are available, and what a given input can recruit — leaving the fast machinery in place.

    Stated as a bet because the nearest measured test of its consequence has already returned a null: precisely timed low-bandwidth intervention in human cortex changed which response the network produced without increasing the dimensionality of its reachable futures. Capacity is not access, and access was the thing we could not measure by counting.

  • Boundary

    “Field” means ordinary membrane state

    Spatially distributed voltage and conductance is already enough to instantiate everything above. No ephaptic mechanism, no new physics, and no holographic storage claim is required or intended. If a successor needs one, it has to earn it separately.

Out of scope

What this does not claim

Kept explicit so a later reader — human or model — does not inherit more than was earned.

  • That a neuron contains an image, a hologram, or a hidden checkerboard.
  • That complicated dendrites are surprising. Dendritic nonlinear computation is established work, not our finding.
  • That any of this explains subjective experience, or that a control architecture implies one.
  • That the disease results from the earlier repos survive. Most did not; the audits are in the history.
  • That morphology is computation. The matched-synapse condition exists precisely because it is not.

The checkerboard was noise. The loop was the clue.

Do not hype. Do not lie. Just show. — every claim on this page carries its standing, and the failures are kept.
